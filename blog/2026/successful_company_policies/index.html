Successful company policies

Created in January 18, 2026

2026

Introduction

This post introduces critical policies to creating a successful business. They are:

  • Feedback Mechanism
  • Core company culture
  • Team bonding
  • Democratic congress (民主会议)

Feedback Mechanism

At Quant-Speed, we have a 谈薪日 at the beginning of every month. The manager and and the employee will be in a private, safe and comfortable room discussing:

  • What the employee thinks their salary should be
  • The employee will reflect on:
    • what work they did well/bad on
    • comment on who they liked best/done the best work
    • what the manager did that is good/bad
  • The manager provide analysis on the employee’s work, and suggestions for improvements

Core Company Values (Most critical)

Countries are birthed from simple and abstract core values - America for freedomn, China for the people (ref. Sapien by Yuval Noah). In detail, the slogan for the two contries are:

  • Freedom!
  • 为人民服务！

respectively. Notably, the ideas are simple and evoke a sense of grandure. This is a powerful tool to bind individuals towards a common goal.

At Quantum-Speed, we follow those two principals and come up with two core values:

  • 世界一流工程师
  • 进入人类AI时代

Observe that these ideas are simple and grandure. I think one of the reason the ideas are so effective is because they leave a huge room for Imagination.

Team bonding

Of course, no body will believe in those core values if they are simply reverberated over and over again. Team bonding is a place where we reinforce those ideas by practicing those ideas. Most critically, to make everyone say those slogans so they believe in them

Democratic congress

The manager writes a speech to reinforce the core values.

A reference speech is a speech from Alexander the great before the soldiers head into battle. In the prior night before the battle, Alexander would consult his commanders on the names of those with significant contribution in the wars. On the day of the battle, Alexander, riding on horse back, would ride infront of the soldier as if randomly and call him out by name and state the soldier’s contribution giving an illusion that he knows all the soldiers by their personal names. He would use that soldier’s contribution as example for his Core Values.
