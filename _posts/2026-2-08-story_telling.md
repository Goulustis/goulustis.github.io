# Introduction
We detail brief template to draw a pie(画大饼). It follows Two steps:
- From high level to low level
- From past to Future

To illustrate the technique, we use Quant-speed's motto - Brining humans to the AI Era

### High level to Low level
The motto - **Bringing human to the AI era** is a very broad and abstract idea. We provide a detail description of the future to exemplify the execution of the abstract idea:

> Imagine in the future, as you walk down the street, instead of interacting with a phone, you will directly talk to an AI agent for directions, to order food, and to find everything you need.

Details give vibrant imagery to the listener, persuading them to believe.

### From Past to Future
> In 1982, a man from Stanford arrived in China and said - no, the internet age has arrived, we should no longer get our information from bill boards and advertisement from newspaper. And so, the Chinese Yellow page is born.

This story directly allures that what we, quant speed, is doing is in parallel to that when the Chinese yellow page is born. We are alluring that, the listener, is witnessing the birth of something interesting.
