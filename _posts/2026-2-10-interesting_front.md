Unlike my other blogs, this one probably will ramble quite a bit as I'm developing the idea while writing.

# Blog
The company needs to be number one at something, but, it doesn't need to be number one in a technical aspect. Since achieving technical prowess in even one aspect seems incredibly unlikely, I propose that we be number one at deployment speed and understand fully the dynamics of a one person company. I propose the dynamics of one person company base on two reasons:
- It is not an aspect that any company will focus on. (wouldn't it be interesting if all members in a company is a one person company)
- The one person company paradigmn is completely new and brings considerable advantage of **SPEED** over traditional organization domain.
    - Could this paradigmn be as revolutionary as Heinz Guderian's proposal to put a radio into every tank (significant increase in communication efficiency)

This come to mind in the brief talk with the CEO of DM, our first incubation company. Their product is incredibly simple. At table top game stores, there may not be sufficient dungeon masters (DMs) to explain an organize a game. AI can easily be in place to assist or replace the DM. This product is incredibly easy for us to build and deply, maybe just two three days. If we can validate and iterate an idea so quickly, then, it is truly a new paradigmn.

I have been struggling to see a path to "bring human to the AI age" since my arrival in the company. But this example is pretty clear, and analogous to Ma in the early Alibaba days, **"让天下没有难做的(硬件)AI"**. If we operate with this goal in mind, technical prowess is pointless, we can be mediocre. We just need to be fastest at deployment and the best with "one person companies".


In economic down turns, the desperation of the people will drive them to try new things. Maybe this is a time for a great social change?
