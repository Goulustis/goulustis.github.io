# The Journey
It all started with a random wechat post from Jeremy. I have noticed his posts from time to time starting from two/three years ago. The initial posts were on teaching how people use ChatGPT. It slowly evolved to case competitions. They all looked like scams. But given that he is the madman I have known him for, I decided to visit.

We had an unforgettable talk the night I visited. He is still as crazy as I known him for. I was shocked that he was able to create such an interesting product, a simple hardware capable of using MCP and speaking in multiple Chinese dialect, in a span of a year-ish. I realized how herculean such a task was given that he did not know how to solder nor code a year ago. 

The company culture also shocked me. They were taking a salary of less than 600USD per month and were willing to work 12+hrs per day. Their eyes had dreams. Their energy was unbelievable. I was left with the question: "what kind of leadership and management can create such a culture?"

I was dejected and depressed at the time due to the poor performance of my company, wire-art studio. I can see that Jeremy has exactly the parts that I lack in running a company. Further, I know people in such culture are happy. Given these two strong motivation, I see no reason not to come. There are so much to be learned.
