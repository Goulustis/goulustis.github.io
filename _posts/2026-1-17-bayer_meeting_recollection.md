We were invited to discuss details about how to train their employees to use Dify. But, our hidden main goal is to influence Bayer to choose us as, possibly, their AI supplier.

# **Identifying interest groups and motivations**
It is of utmost importance to identify how many camps are in the room and what their motivations to direct us on how to navigate the discussion to our goal. There are three notable camps in the room:
- 深总(For bervity, Mr.Shen) - the head of Bayer at Kunming
- The factory camp
- The office camp

## **Motivation Analysis**
### <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 0);">Mr.Shen</span>
We analyze Mr.Shen from 2 aspects - Age and position in the company. He is in is late 40s and at the top position in Kunming. Due to his age, he is at his late stage of his career. He is just at a point in his age that he can climb one more step up the corporate ladder before retirement. He needs a notible achievement(政绩) to justify his promotion. Hence he is very motivated to introduce AI into Bayer. He is **a strong supporting camp.**


**The German Superior** - he is one level above Mr.Shen. We had intel that he was in Kunming, but why isn't he there? Because Mr.Shen wants to:
- make sure we can actually do work
- we are **His People** when we meet Mr.Shen to bolster his achievement

### <span style="color:rgb(255, 0, 221);">The Office Camp</span>
We analyze the office camp from the nature of their work. Part of their task involves data visualization via powerBI - easily replacible by AI today. Hence, they are threatened by our arrival. How the head of the department questions our data visualization further corroborates our hypothesis. He attempts to find all avenues of flaws in our demonstrations.

**Mr.Shen Notes** Mr.Shen is extremely smart. When the office head critisize the AI visualizations, Mr. Shen observes that the office head was threatened and **IMMEDATELY** comforts him by saying: "when we move forward with the visualization ai-fication, you'll be in charge of the change". GENIUS!

**Update Note** - After dinner with our mole, the office head appears to be a smart scholar. Trained as a researcher myself, I see his need to absolutely confirm that there is an advantage in switching to AI before implementation - local optima are rare.

So, it is hard to determine at this point. But, we will stick to the conclusion that this is an **opposing camp.** 

### <span style="color:rgb(255, 255, 0);">The Factory Camp</span>
Similar to the office camp, we analyze them by the nature of their work. Early on the meeting, we saw excitement when we mentioned computer vision (CV). This implies that they focus on efficiency and are open to introduce AI related technology to help them. Hence when we mentioned using multi-modal AI to aid them, they were extremely excited. Hence, this is a **strong support camp**.

## **Our Moves**
Now that the hidden motivation of all the players are revealed. How we persuade them in future meetings is clear. We will align ourselves strongly with both Mr.Shen and the factory camp and use our mole from the office camp to change the office camp's mind.

# **On Answering Questions**
**Answer the actual need of the quetioner and not the question asked**. In the beginning of the meeting, Mr.Shen intelligently verbalize all his concerns. One of which is whether they should use Dify or not. Our opinion is that they should not because Dify is too clunky. We stopped using it some months ago. 

With deeper observation, the question on Dify usage is actually "whether Dify will actually solve their existing problem". Gan was observant to realize this, so he did not answer this question at all but instead expand on the capability of AI instead - leading into a small discussion on multi-modal AI.

**Personal Note -** I think this is actually the fault of the questioner's capability to ask questions as he was incapable of identifying the most valuable piece of information he needs for quality decision making.


# **My Blunder**
I made a mistake of attempting Mr.Shen, the **LEADER**, infront of his underlings. Doing so damages his reputation. Further, proving him wrong does not prove us better than those at Bayer - so correcting him is a **USELESS** and **HARMFUL** action.

Early on the meeting, Mr.Shen mentioned that AI was capable of solving unsolved math problems. That was incorrect as I recall that progress were made to those math problems in 1920s. It was a case of test data leakage. As I just started to correct him, he shuts me and states that math is not of a concern - at the same time, comforting me that I'm not entirely wrong to respect my expertise. Doing so saves his face and respecting my dignity at the same time. Mr.Shen is so so brilliant.

# **After Note**
It was a brilliant meeting in everyway. After the meeting, Mr.Shen personally returned to the meeting room and took our panflet. It is a great sign. We will attempt to analyze their pain point and come up with a demo by the end of the Dify training. It is quite hopeful that we can achieve some sort of collaboration.
